# Design

- Prefers conventional auth call-to-action labels ("Sign In" / "Sign Up") over marketing phrasing like "Get Started" — reverted "Get Started" back to "Sign Up" in the navbar. Confidence: 0.6
- Expects pages to be fully responsive across all device sizes (mobile/tablet/desktop), not just desktop — stated as a standing rule for every page and told the assistant to "remember it". Confidence: 0.85
- Wants parallel pages (e.g., sign-in and sign-up) to share the same layout structure: once one page's design is approved, expects its sibling to mirror it exactly rather than take a different approach. Confidence: 0.75
- Prefers photos to fill their container edge-to-edge (cover) with the image and adjacent text balanced, rather than being letterboxed/shrunk to fit with visible background showing. Confidence: 0.7
- Expects new sections to reuse the app's established brand palette (e.g., the same orange→red gradient and white text used on the auth pages), keeping colors consistent across pages. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers marketing/hero sections to be minimal so they don't compete with the primary content below (wanted a slim banner so the menu cards stay the focus). Confidence: 0.6
